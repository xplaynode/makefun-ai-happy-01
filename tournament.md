# ai-happy-01 — local pairwise tournament (judge-rubric replica)

Method: single-elimination bracket over the 16 generated candidates, judged in the
persona of the sealed make.fun judge from RULES.txt. For each pairing the criteria are
applied **in the judge's order** — 1) makes you happy (instant warmth/delight/joy),
2) the idea (fresh, specific moment/gesture/surprise; stock smiley / generic sunny scene
marked down), 3) feeling (colour, light, expression, composition pull to one mood),
4) craft (clean, no artefacts, no broken anatomy), 5) reads small at ~300 px — and the
first criterion where the pair differs decides, unless a later one differs by much more.
Margins use the judge's 1–5 scale.

Per-candidate notes from viewing (all 16 viewed at full size):

## Round of 16
- C02 def. C14 (margin 4) — unambiguous laughter vs a mouth that can read as a snarl; criterion 1 decides at once.
- C08 def. C12 (margin 4) — explosive joy vs serene calm; criterion 1.
- C01 def. C10 (margin 3) — focused tender warmth vs awe where the happy subject is tiny; criteria 1–2.
- C13 def. C05 (margin 2) — both tender gestures; C05's faces carry a wistful tint, C13's sloth is openly grinning; criterion 1.
- C15 def. C04 (margin 3) — joy in motion beats cozy serenity; C04's cool palette also loses criterion 3.
- C09 def. C11 (margin 2) — photographic bliss and a cleaner read; C11's tail-flame is a craft risk; criteria 1, 4.
- C06 def. C07 (margin 2) — proud party grin + confetti colour beats gentle wonder; criterion 1.
- C16 def. C03 (margin 2) — bolder idea and small-read; C03 is nearer the generic-cute the rubric marks down; criteria 2, 5.

## Quarter-finals
- C02 def. C16 (margin 2) — C16's gag may edge criterion 2, but criterion 1 comes first: C02's open-mouthed laugh plus paw-holding is the bigger instant lift; C16's hero looks earnest, not joyful.
- C08 def. C09 (margin 2) — both ecstatic; C08 adds motion, orange-on-snow contrast and a laughing face vs C09's quiet bliss; criteria 1, 3.
- C01 def. C06 (margin 2) — C06 grins faster, but C01 is the more distinctive, memorable idea (criterion 2) with flawless light and craft (3, 4) — the later criteria differ by more than the first.
- C15 def. C13 (margin 2) — C13 owns the cleverest idea, but C15 is the bigger hit of pure joy (criterion 1) in a cleaner frame; C13's tortoise claw is small.

## Semi-finals
- C02 def. C08 (margin 1) — the final-quality match of the bracket. Both are laughter-forward, warm, clean and read perfectly small. Criterion 1 is nearly level (solo kinetic ecstasy vs shared laughter); criterion 2 tips it: holding paws so they don't drift apart is a *story* — two joys, doubled expressions, one gesture.
- C01 def. C15 (margin 2) — C15 is joyful but "happy animal in a meadow" is a familiar scene; C01's robot-and-sunflower is singular, with superior light, craft and narrative (criteria 2–4 differ by more than C15's edge in exuberance).

## Final
- **C02 def. C01 (margin 2) — WINNER: C02, the otter pair.** On criterion 1, applied first: both images lift the mood at a glance, but C02's lift is *laughter* — the most contagious and unambiguous happiness signal a vision judge can receive — doubled across two faces and sealed by the paw-holding gesture, in wall-to-wall golden sparkle (criterion 3). C01 is the more beautiful and more original image, and it wins criteria 2 and 4; but its happiness is gentle awe, and its hero's face is two dark eyes — less expressive. The first criterion differs by a real margin, so it decides.
- **Runner-up: C01, the robot gardener.**

Final ranking of the top four: 1) C02 otters, 2) C01 robot gardener, 3) C08 red panda slide, 4) C15 goat leap.
