# ai-happy-01 — candidate images

Competition: "Make the AI judge happy" (make.fun, program/v1 image contest).
Judge rubric (from RULES.txt, in priority order):
1. **It makes you happy** — one look lifts the mood; real warmth, delight or joy. A cold image loses to any that makes you smile.
2. **The idea** — a fresh, specific reason to be happy: a moment, a gesture, a surprise of its own. NOT a stock smiley face or generic sunny scene.
3. **Feeling** — colour, light, expression and composition all pull toward one happy mood.
4. **Craft** — clean render in its chosen style; no smears, artefacts, garbled text, or accidentally broken anatomy/perspective. (All prompts below avoid text and human hands.)
5. **Reads small** — at ~300 px across the main shape and idea must still be clear. (All prompts: one dominant subject, simple composition.)

Limits: PNG/JPEG, ≤ 1 MiB, ≤ 1024×1024. Entries whose file is byte-identical to another are merged, and the kit's starter pixel-sun must NOT be reused.
